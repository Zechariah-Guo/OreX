About OreX

What is OreX?

OreX is a Minecraft-themed stock market simulation where you trade ores like stocks. Each ore has unique market characteristics — from stable Coal to volatile Netherite. Prices fluctuate every 20 seconds based on an algorithm that considers trends, supply and demand, bot traders, and random market events.

The 9 Ores

Coal

Volatility: 0.5

Stable and low value. A reliable fuel source found abundantly throughout the world. Low risk, low reward.

Iron

Volatility: 0.6

Reliable and moderate. The backbone of any toolkit, always in demand. A solid mid-range investment.

Copper

Volatility: 0.8

Slightly volatile. A versatile metal with unpredictable market swings.

Gold

Volatility: 0.9

Mid-tier and balanced. Prized for its lustre and consistent trading value.

Lapis Lazuli

Volatility: 1.0

Unpredictable. A mystical gem whose value shifts with the winds of fortune.

Redstone

Volatility: 1.2

High risk. Powers complex machinery but its market is notoriously unstable.

Emerald

Volatility: 0.9

Premium with moderate volatility. The currency of villagers, valued for its rarity.

Diamond

High value, relatively stable. The pinnacle of mining, coveted by all.

Netherite

Volatility: 1.4

Highest value, very volatile. Forged in the depths of the Nether, extremely rare. High risk, high reward.

Market Mechanics

The market engine uses several factors to determine price movements:

  • Trend effect — If a price has been rising, it becomes more likely to fall (mean reversion), and vice versa.
  • Gravity effect — Prices that drift far from their base price are pulled back over time.
  • Bot traders — 9 simulated traders make decisions each tick, creating market activity.
  • Player influence — Your trades have a small effect on the next price movement.
  • Market events — Rare events (0.5% chance per tick) cause sudden 3× price movements.
  • Volatility — Each ore has a volatility rating that scales price changes.
