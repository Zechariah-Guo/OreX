About OreX

What is OreX?

OreX is a gamified stock market simulation with Minecraft-inspired ores, where you trade ores like stocks. Each ore has unique market characteristics, from stable Coal to volatile Netherite. Prices fluctuate every 20 seconds based on an algorithm that considers trends, supply and demand, bot traders, and random market events.

The 9 Ores

Coal

Volatility: 0.5

Stable and low value. A reliable fuel source found abundantly throughout the world.

Iron

Volatility: 0.6

Reliable and moderate. The backbone of any recipe, always in demand.

Copper

Volatility: 0.8

Slightly volatile. A versatile metal with growing use cases and unpredictable market demand.

Gold

Volatility: 0.9

Mid-tier and balanced. Prized for its usefulness and popularity with piglins.

Lapis Lazuli

Volatility: 1.0

Unpredictable. A mystical gem whose value shifts on the whims of enchanters.

Redstone

Volatility: 1.2

High risk. Needed for automation and complex machines, but there can easily be an over-supply.

Emerald

Volatility: 0.9

Premium with moderate volatility. The currency of villagers, valued for its rarity and liquidity.

Diamond

Volatility: 0.7

High value, relatively stable. The pinnacle of mining, coveted by all for all manner of recipes.

Netherite

Volatility: 1.4

Highest value, very volatile. Forged in the depths of the Nether, extremely rare.

Market Mechanics

The market engine uses several factors to determine price movements:

Trend Effect

If a price has been rising, it becomes more likely to fall (mean reversion), and vice versa.

Gravity Effect

Prices that drift far from their base price are pulled back over time.

Bot Traders

9 simulated traders make decisions each tick, creating realistic market activity.

Player Influence

Your trades have a small effect on the next price movement.

Market Events

Rare events (0.5% chance per tick) cause sudden 3× price movements.

Volatility

Each ore has a volatility rating that scales price changes — from stable Coal (0.5) to wild Netherite (1.4).
